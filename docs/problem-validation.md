# Sprint 1 – Problem Validation (Capstone 2)

## Overview

This document records the validation of the project's problem statement during Sprint 1. The goal was to confirm that the problem being solved is real, appropriately scoped, and aligned with the Product Owner's expectations before major development begins.

---

## Original Problem

College students who regularly exercise often use multiple applications to manage workouts, nutrition, and wellness information. Because their progress is spread across several platforms, it becomes difficult to quickly determine whether they are making meaningful progress toward their fitness goals.

---

## Product Owner Feedback

The Product Owner agreed that the identified problem represents a realistic issue for the target user.

However, rather than attempting to solve every aspect of personal health management during the first sprint, the Product Owner recommended focusing on the smallest valuable workflow that demonstrates the concept.

The primary recommendation was to validate the core experience before expanding into additional functionality.

---

## Changes Made

Based on the discussion, the following changes were made:

- Narrowed the Sprint 1 scope to a single end-to-end workflow.
- Prioritized workout tracking over nutrition and wellness features.
- Delayed AI recommendations until a later sprint.
- Delayed wearable device integration until after the MVP.
- Focused development on demonstrating immediate workout progress after logging a workout.

---

## Validated User

**Alex**

A college student balancing coursework, a part-time job, and regular exercise who wants to quickly determine whether his workouts are helping him reach his fitness goals without switching between multiple applications.

---

## Sprint 1 Goal

By the end of Sprint 1, the application will demonstrate a working end-to-end flow where Alex can:

1. Log into the application.
2. Record a workout.
3. Save the workout.
4. Immediately view updated progress on the dashboard.

---

## Validation Outcome

The Product Owner approved the revised problem statement and agreed that the Sprint 1 scope appropriately addresses the highest-priority user need while keeping the implementation realistic for an initial sprint.
